# Key Learnings

- Apex is used for advanced Salesforce customization.
- Flows are useful for simple automation tasks.
- Enterprise systems often require programming for flexibility.
- Pseudocode helps in understanding logic before coding.
- Apex supports integrations with external systems.

---

# Practical Understanding

I understood how Salesforce combines declarative tools and programming to build scalable enterprise systems. I also learned that coding becomes necessary when business logic becomes complex.

---

# Real World Understanding

In a College Management System:
- Flow can automate notifications
- Validation Rules maintain data quality
- Formula Fields calculate values automatically
- Apex handles advanced eligibility and payment logic

This helped me understand how all Salesforce concepts connect together in real enterprise applications.
