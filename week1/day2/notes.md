
# Day 2 Notes - Salesforce Platform Basics

## What is Salesforce Platform?
Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used to manage customer data, sales, service, automation, and application development.

---

## App in Salesforce
An App is a collection of tabs, objects, and tools designed for a specific business process.

### Examples
- Sales App
- Service App

---

## Object in Salesforce
Objects are database tables used to store data.

### Types
1. Standard Objects
   - Account
   - Contact
   - Lead

2. Custom Objects
   - Student
   - Patient

---

## Tab in Salesforce
Tabs help users access objects and records through the Salesforce UI.

### Examples
- Account Tab
- Contact Tab

---

## Configuration vs Coding

### Configuration
- No-code or low-code development
- Uses Flow, Validation Rules, Process Builder

### Coding
- Uses Apex and Lightning Web Components
- Used for complex business logic

---

## Multi-Tenant Architecture
Multiple organizations share the same Salesforce infrastructure securely.

---

## System Design Example - College Management
### App
College Management App

### Objects
- Student
- Faculty
- Course

### User Interaction
Students register for courses and faculty manage attendance.
